# 4.4: Quality Management System and its Processes

### 4.4.1
We use customer feedback to improve the processes and quality management system.

### 4.4.2
We maintain documented information in support of SRSG processes through collecting feedback from customers.

Collecting feedback is important to understanding whether we have met the customer’s requirements. We collect feedback during the project and at the end of the project. The scale of feedback collection is dependent on the scale of the project - for a smaller software project (in the range of days - weeks), this might involve simply checking in verbally with the customer during a meeting to; for a larger software project, training course or event, this might involve a more formal feedback method such as a feedback survey.
